# MFG-03 — Company and Staff Accounts

Implementation specification · Complete-system scope. Traceability: [function list](../docs/function-list.md), rows 17–24; shared contracts D01–D03 and D11 in [system decisions](../docs/system-decisions.md); factual provenance: [user input register](../docs/user-input-needed.md).

## Purpose, permissions, and entities

Only System Admin provisions Company records and staff Memberships. CompanyAccount is a Company plus staff memberships; customer identity/capability is separate. Staff roles are per-company Membership records (Company Admin or Sales Consultant), System Admin is a system capability, and customers may belong to multiple companies through their business records. Never model these as a single global User.role or accept role/company_id from the client as authority. System Admin has no implied access to customer designs, order mutations or company financial exports.

`Company(id:UUID,name,tax_id,address,admin_contact_info,status:Provisioning|Active|Suspended|Deleted,created_at,updated_at,version)`; `Membership(id,company_id,user_id,role,active,created_at,updated_at,version)`; `Invitation(id,company_id,email,role,token_hash,expires_at,accepted_at?,version)`. Creation atomically creates a Provisioning company, inactive pending initial Company Admin membership and its 48-hour single-use invitation. Company becomes Active only after invitation acceptance. Acceptance with a new identity uses S03 invitation mode to set full_name/password and consume/verify invite; existing identity authenticates first, then accepts. Acceptance atomically creates/links identity, activates membership and consumes token. Email is globally unique; duplicate/conflicting active membership 409. No temporary password. Company name 1–150, tax_id 1–30, address 1–250, contact name 1–100 and normalized email. Company lifecycle: Provisioning→Active→Suspended→Active; provisioning/active/suspended may reach Deleted only when open-work checks pass. Suspension blocks new catalog/order/payment activity while permitting customer reads and payment reconciliation.

Protect the last active System Admin and the last active Company Admin in an active company against demotion, deactivation or deletion. All deletion is soft, revokes sessions, preserves historical business records. Resolve open work by reactivation before deleting the company. For staff membership removal, reassign consultant-owned open consultations/design requests first; it need not delete the company or user identity.

## Acceptance and screen behavior

- Valid company creation yields exactly one Provisioning company, pending initial-admin Membership, hashed invitation and outbox delivery; retry returns same records.
- Company remains unavailable for catalog/order/payment activity until initial admin accepts; expired/replayed invite cannot activate it.
- New staff sets name/password in S03 invitation mode; existing user must authenticate before membership acceptance. Acceptance atomically consumes invite and activates correct company role.
- Concurrent activation/invitation and account edits serialize; one version wins and stale operations return 409.
- Last active system/company admin cannot be removed; cross-company membership never grants access to another company.
- Suspending a company blocks new catalog/order/payment but preserves customer reads and payment reconciliation. Company deletion rejects open work; membership removal rejects unassigned open consultant work until reassigned.
- Membership removal preserves user identity and other memberships; company deletion preserves historical records and revokes affected sessions.

S41 is Company Accounts: list/detail/create/invite/edit/suspend and separate company vs membership deletion flows. Loading, empty, forbidden, error, success and retry states follow D11; invitation email failure does not roll back transaction and remains retryable. All eight F-ACC IDs map one-to-one to FR-001..008.
